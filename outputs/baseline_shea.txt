Baseline Response:
Here is your regulatory compliance advisory for launching your **Shea Butter Body Cream** in Lagos.

---

### 1. Primary Regulatory Body Responsible
* **NAFDAC (National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control):** Primary authority governing the manufacture, importation, advertisement, distribution, and sale of cosmetics in 
Nigeria under the *Cosmetics and Toiletries (Registration) Regulations*.

---

### 2. Required Permits and Registrations

#### **A. Corporate & Intellectual Property Setup**
1. **CAC (Corporate Affairs Commission):** Register your business as a **Limited Liability Company (Ltd)** or a **Business Name**. 
   * *Advice:* An "Ltd" is strongly recommended if you intend to scale, stock in major supermarkets, or secure corporate funding.
2. **Trademark Registration:** File for trademark protection with the *Commercial Law Department, Federal Ministry of Industry, Trade and Investment* to secure your brand name and logo before 
submitting labels to NAFDAC.

#### **B. NAFDAC Product Registration (Cosmetics Scheme)**
1. **Facility Inspection & Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) Approval:** NAFDAC officers must inspect your production venue to ensure it meets cosmetic manufacturing hygiene standards.
2. **Product Laboratory Analysis:** Sample testing for safety parameters (microbiology, heavy metals, stability, and pH).
3. **Product Registration / Listing Certificate:** Granting a NAFDAC Registration Number (mandatory to print on product packaging).
   * *Note for Small Scale:* You can leverage NAFDAC’s **MSME Fast-Track / Cottage Industry Scheme**, which offers reduced fees and simpler facility requirements for small-batch producers.

#### **C. Other Relevant Bodies**
* **SON (Standards Organisation of Nigeria):** Mandatory Conformity Assessment Programme (**MANCAP**) certification for locally manufactured goods.
* **LASEPA (Lagos State Environmental Protection Agency):** Required if operating a standalone factory in Lagos (waste disposal and environmental audit compliance).
* **LIRS & FIRS:** Tax Identification Numbers (TIN) for state and federal tax compliance.

---

### 3. Key Compliance Risks & Flags

* **Medical / Therapeutic Claims (High Risk):** 
  * *Flag:* Do **not** use claims like *"cures eczema," "treats hyperpigmentation," "relieves arthritis,"* or *"anti-fungal."* 
  * *Consequence:* NAFDAC will reject your application or classify the product as a **Drug/Medicament**, which requires far stricter, more expensive clinical trials. Stick to cosmetic claims: 
*"moisturizes," "softens skin," "promotes smooth skin."*
* **Home Kitchen Production Rules:** 
  * *Flag:* Manufacturing directly in your home kitchen is prohibited unless you have a designated, isolated space that meets NAFDAC Cottage Industry guidelines (seamless flooring, washable 
walls, pest control, dedicated changing areas, stainless steel mixing equipment).
* **Banned Ingredients & Heavy Metals:** 
  * Ensure raw additives (e.g., fragrances, preservatives, oils) do not contain hydroquinone, steroids, mercury, or restricted parabens. NAFDAC lab analysis *will* detect these.
* **Strict Labeling Guidelines:**
  * Labels must include: Full ingredient list (using INCI nomenclature), net volume/weight, manufacturing & expiry dates, batch number, manufacturer’s address, storage conditions, and a 
dedicated space for the **NAFDAC Reg. No.**
